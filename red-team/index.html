THE RED-TEAM DOOR

Break something.

The most valuable contribution to this programme is a good attack. Here is what is attackable today, how to swing, and what happens when you land one.

Attackable now: the paper’s claims and statistics, the frozen scoring key, the Clause and its reasons, the Keeping Map and every argument on this site. If a sentence here would not survive your seminar, we want the seminar.

How to swing: email hello@themeaningmotive.org with the subject break. House rules, and they cut both ways: attack the strongest version of the claim, not the easiest; bring receipts; aim at the work, never the people.

What happens when you land one: the correction goes in the Evidence Room’s log, with your name on it unless you prefer otherwise. Around here the corrections log is the trophy cabinet.

Coming next: when the second battery’s design freezes, it publishes here with its hash before a single trial runs. That is the invitation: attack the experiment while it can still be improved, not after it can only be defended.
