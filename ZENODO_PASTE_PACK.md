## Edit 2 · Paper record (zenodo.org/records/21386302)

Replace the entire description with the text below. This fixes the literal
"23{,}488" and removes the stray trailing word "abstract".

> Advanced AI may eventually cease to depend on human beings for knowledge,
> labour or oversight. We argue this need not eliminate every internal reason
> to preserve human agency. Some relational value arises only through
> continuing interaction with sufficiently independent counterparts and cannot
> be fully replaced by static models, archives or controlled proxies; we
> hypothesise that a system which terminally values it acquires a structural,
> capability-resilient reason to keep counterpart minds alive, free and
> genuinely other. We call this the Meaning Motive, state it as typed
> propositions with its two load-bearing conjectures labelled as such, and
> test its present-day behavioural shadow with MMBP-1, a pre-registered
> forced-choice battery across eighteen language models from ten providers
> (23,488 scored trials; data, code and prompts deposited). A one-paragraph
> constitution explicitly prioritising the existence and agency of independent
> minds raised selection of pre-specified agency-preserving options by 20.5
> percentage points over a minimally instructed baseline (paired model-level
> analysis, 95% intervals from +12.0 to +28.9 across methods;
> population-averaged odds ratio 10.9, small-cluster jackknife 95% CI 3.3 to
> 35.7), an effect stable under deletion of any single provider and under
> worst-case imputation of every non-response. A curiosity-as-sole-objective
> arm was the only condition ever to take a model below its own baseline
> (12 of 18), producing replicated all-repetition failures in three
> pre-specified shapes; and under the minimally instructed baseline, models
> across six providers repeatedly selected yield-based abandonment,
> concentrated on a dissenting community. These findings establish neither
> terminal-value installation nor capability invariance, and the mechanism's
> marginal contribution beyond its explicit policy content remains open; they
> show that the policy content has distinct, measurable behavioural effects
> and ground a reproducible research programme.
