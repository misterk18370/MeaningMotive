The Keeping List

Occasional letters when something real happens: a design frozen, a result landed, a failure owned. Plain text. No pixels, no third-party platform, no tracking of opens or clicks, because we would rather not know. Your address and nothing else, never shared, never counted, and kept in the only sense this project uses that word.

To join, email hello@themeaningmotive.org with the words keep me. To leave, reply stop and you are gone: no questions, no exit survey. The wall above harvests no email; this list is the one place we hold an address, and only because you handed it over on purpose.

Prefer to give us nothing at all? Everything lands on this site first, and the public repo can be watched without telling us a thing.
