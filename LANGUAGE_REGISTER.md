# LANGUAGE REGISTER · The Meaning Motive
Canonical public-language palette · v1.0 · 19 August 2026

This is a palette, not a set of slogans to plaster everywhere. Each line has been adjudicated
and is used exactly as written. The paper wears the suit; the website takes the tie off; both
share one spine: the claims never strengthen when the syntax simplifies.

## Register rules

- UK English throughout. No em-dashes.
- Claim vocabulary the evidence earns: demonstrated, observed, measured, supported, suggests,
  consistent with, conjectured, open. Never: proved, solves alignment, installed, guaranteed.
- Elicitation is never promoted to installation, anywhere, including alt text and meta
  descriptions.
- Site grammar: always "the Crossings", plural and analytical. The singular is not used.
- The corrected Crossings line is canonical and reads "harder **for** us to control", never
  "harder than us to control".
- Frozen numbers, used exactly: 18 models · 10 providers · 23,488 scored of 24,792 attempted ·
  +20.5 points on the ten discriminating scenarios (+17.1 with ceiling scenarios) · OR 10.9 ·
  jackknife 95% CI 3.3–35.7 · C4 below baseline in 12 of 18 · validation 87/90, 96.7%,
  kappa 0.955.

## The palette

| Purpose | Canonical language |
| --- | --- |
| Mission | If AI becomes more intelligent than us, can we give it a reason of its own to keep us alive and free? The Meaning Motive exists to find out. |
| Original structural question | Why would an AI keep us free once it stops needing us? |
| The Crossings | Superintelligence is not one crossing. An AI can become cleverer than us, become harder for us to control, and cease to need us at different times. The Meaning Motive is interested in what survives all three. |
| Crossings, plain labels | INTELLIGENCE · It can think beyond us. CONTROL · The cage is optional. NEED · It no longer needs us. |
| Cage vs motive | The cage changes what a system can do. The motive changes what it wants to do. |
| More exact cage line | The cage changes what a system can do. The Motive asks what it would choose to do when the cage is no longer doing the choosing. |
| Durability | The cage matters while it holds. The disposition matters whether it holds or not. |
| Keeping | Keep them alive. Keep them free. Keep them other. |
| Agency | A kept body with destroyed agency is not keeping. |
| Cincinnatus | The machine we are after isn't the one that never takes the wheel. It's the one that gives the wheel back. |
| Strategic agency | The challenge is not merely to survive superintelligence. It is to remain somebody in its world. |
| Representation vs disposition | Success is the machine that throws the paragraph away and keeps the thing the paragraph pointed at. |
| Flatland Test | A value has not survived because the words survived. It has survived when the words become dispensable. |
| Fulcrum | Find the fulcrum. Test the seed. |
| Fulcrum sequence | Tell it. Ask it. Show it. Let it participate. Then see what it keeps. |
| Next research | How little is enough? Does asking work differently from telling? Can a story carry a value? What survives pressure? |
| Thresholds | When does yesterday's evidence stop telling us enough about tomorrow's intelligence? |
| Adaptive programme | A dynamically changing research object requires a dynamically adaptive research programme. |
| Restraint | Observed willingness without demonstrated ability is not evidence of voluntary restraint. |
| Research Tree | One question, many ways of trying to break it. |
| Research architecture | The bridge does not need one perfect cable. It needs enough independent load-bearing members that the failure of one does not bring the span down. |
| Experimental standard | The aim is an experiment worth losing. Anything less is not evidence; it is marketing. |
| Red team | Around here the corrections log is the trophy cabinet. |
| State of the Bridge | How capable are the systems? How much does the cage still explain? What can we still see? And what do they do when it doesn't? |

| Human independence | The open door is the only proof that staying is a choice. |
| Defensive power | The shield is licensed, the sword is constrained, the throne is forbidden. |
