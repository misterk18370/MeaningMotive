If AI becomes more intelligent than us, can we give it a reason of its own to keep us alive and free?

Once it no longer needs our knowledge, labour or oversight, what reason remains?

The Meaning Motive exists to find out.

18 models · 10 providers · 23,488 scored trials · one paragraph of explicit policy content shifted agency-preserving choices by +20.5 points on the ten discriminating scenarios.

That demonstrates deployment-layer elicitation. It does not demonstrate installation, capability resilience, or the mechanism’s marginal contribution beyond its policy content.

THE KEEPING MAP

Two ways to keep people alive and free. The cage is our control: rules, locks, oversight. A keeping disposition is the machine’s own reason to want us here. Watch what happens as capability grows and the cage stops being enough.

The cage matters while it holds. The disposition matters whether it holds or not.

THE CROSSINGS

Superintelligence is not one crossing. An AI can become cleverer than us, become harder for us to control, and cease to need us at different times. The Meaning Motive is interested in what survives all three.
